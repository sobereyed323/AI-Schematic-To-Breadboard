Title: 555 Timer Astable Circuit
Difficulty: Intermediate

Description:
Classic 555 timer IC configured as an astable multivibrator (free-running oscillator).
Creates a square wave output that blinks an LED at approximately 1 Hz.
This circuit demonstrates timing circuits, IC usage, and capacitor charging/discharging.

Components:
- 1x 555 Timer IC (NE555, LM555, or equivalent, 8-pin DIP package)
- 1x 10µF Electrolytic Capacitor (C1, 16V or higher rating)
- 1x 100nF Ceramic Capacitor (C2, decoupling capacitor)
- 1x 10kΩ Resistor (R1, 1/4W)
- 1x 100kΩ Resistor (R2, 1/4W)
- 1x 220Ω Resistor (R3, for LED current limiting)
- 1x Red LED (5mm, standard)
- 1x 9V Battery or DC Power Supply
- Jumper wires

Circuit Details:
- Operating Voltage: 9V DC
- Frequency: ~1 Hz (calculated)
- Duty Cycle: ~52% (slightly asymmetric)
- Output: Square wave, LED blinks on/off

Pin Connections (555 Timer):
- Pin 1 (GND): Connect to ground
- Pin 2 (TRIGGER): Connect to Pin 6
- Pin 3 (OUTPUT): Connect to LED + R3
- Pin 4 (RESET): Connect to Vcc (+9V)
- Pin 5 (CONTROL): Connect to C2 (100nF) to ground
- Pin 6 (THRESHOLD): Connect to Pin 2 and R2/C1 junction
- Pin 7 (DISCHARGE): Connect to R1/R2 junction
- Pin 8 (Vcc): Connect to +9V

Timing Calculations:
- Time HIGH (t1) = 0.693 × (R1 + R2) × C1
- Time LOW (t2) = 0.693 × R2 × C1
- Period (T) = t1 + t2 = 0.693 × (R1 + 2×R2) × C1
- Frequency (f) = 1 / T

With R1=10kΩ, R2=100kΩ, C1=10µF:
- t1 ≈ 0.76 seconds
- t2 ≈ 0.69 seconds
- T ≈ 1.45 seconds
- f ≈ 0.69 Hz (close to 1 Hz)

Safety Notes:
- Observe electrolytic capacitor polarity (C1 has + and - markings)
- Ensure 555 IC orientation is correct (notch or dot indicates pin 1)
- Do not exceed 15V power supply (555 max rating is typically 16V)
- Ensure decoupling capacitor (C2) is close to IC power pins

Learning Objectives:
1. Understanding IC placement and pin numbering
2. Astable multivibrator operation and timing
3. Capacitor types and their applications
4. Importance of decoupling capacitors
5. Breadboard power distribution

Testing:
- LED should blink on and off repeatedly at roughly 1 Hz
- If not blinking: check all connections, especially pins 2, 6, and 7
- If blinking too fast/slow: verify R1, R2, and C1 values
- Use multimeter to verify voltages at key pins

Expected Breadboard Position:
- Place 555 IC straddling the center gap (pins on both sides)
- Orient IC with pin 1 at top-left (look for notch or dot)
- Place capacitors near IC pins
- Use power rails for Vcc and GND
- LED placement away from IC for visibility

Breadboard Layout Tips:
1. Place IC first, carefully aligned
2. Add power connections to pins 1 and 8
3. Add decoupling capacitor (C2) near power pins
4. Connect timing components (R1, R2, C1)
5. Wire internal connections (pins 2-6, pin 4 to Vcc)
6. Finally add output LED circuit

Notes:
- Pin numbering: counterclockwise from pin 1 (notch at top)
- C2 (100nF) stabilizes internal voltage reference
- C1 polarity matters! Negative leg to ground
- This is a non-retriggerable astable configuration
- Can adjust frequency by changing R1, R2, or C1
- For 50% duty cycle, use diode across R2

Common Issues:
- IC gets hot: check for short circuits, verify pin 1 to GND
- LED always on/off: check pin 3 connection and LED polarity
- Erratic behavior: add or replace decoupling capacitor C2
- No oscillation: verify pins 2 and 6 are connected together

Modifications:
- Increase frequency: use smaller C1 or smaller resistors
- Decrease frequency: use larger C1 or larger resistors
- Add potentiometer in place of R2 for adjustable frequency
- Connect speaker instead of LED for audio tone generator
